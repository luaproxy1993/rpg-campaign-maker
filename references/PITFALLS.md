# Pitfalls (from a real one-sitting slice)

These are the failures that made a "small AVG" take a production's worth of loops. Read when something looks done and the author is still unhappy.

## Lock

- Shipping past the named stop (the Lettow-class character) because the book continues.
- Putting a flashback NPC in the live companion slot.
- Writing a 3-minute accept that only describes mood, not a system firing.

## Voice

- Translating English ChoiceScript into "cinematic" Chinese. Players who know the book bounce immediately.
- Shortening long choice labels. The length **is** the tree.

## Tree

- One layer of choices, all merging. Feels like a slideshow.
- Stats that never appear in a check.
- Items granted and never tested.

## Art

- Keying on a textured rose background — the key eats the figure.
- Hard-keying all magenta-ish pixels — purple dresses and blood vanish.
- Checking cutouts on black — leftover pink looks like shadow.
- Leaving generation dust/sand in the PNG instead of as scene FX.
- Regenerating faces to "smooth card" style when the brief was oil-brush restyle of the original face.

## FX

- One sand overlay on every scene, including interiors and night sky.
- Particles drawn **under** the UI vignette — only the opening windshield stars remain visible.
- Stars drawn over dashboards; sand drawn over portraits.

## Audio

- Synthesized triangle-wave UI clicks on an oil-painting game.
- Official OSTs dropped in "for vibe".

## Shell

- Generic VN chrome when the author named a specific commercial layout.
- Cache not bumped; the author is still seeing the pink PNG.
- Julian-class companion, Chinese copy, missing start plate: three different "this is not the game I asked for" reports. Each is a lock/voice/speaker bug, not a missing feature.

## Media (Candy-class)

- A chat window plus generated pics/video, labeled a campaign. That is a **companion OS**, not a sitting. See `MEDIA-LAYERS.md`.
- Play-time PixVerse / I2V behind `play/`. Video beats are a **clip-library** on the tree.
- Treating Roulette/Cam “live” as realtime gen. The live *feel* is a looping head or a clip swap; the menu is finite.
- Explicit sexual clips through the PixVerse key. Policy fail. Author-time self-hosted I2V, then clip-library, or omit.
- “She randomly sent a selfie” as model emergence. It is a clock or a node.
